# Audience profile

One per audience. A learning platform has at least two: learners and
administrators.

```markdown
Audience:
Who they are:
What they are trying to get done:
What they know:
What they do not know: <for example: CDN, UUID, endpoint, file path>
Their words: <labels and terms the interface must use>
Context: <devices, frequency, connection, time pressure>
Accessibility needs: <default WCAG 2.2 AA, plus anything specific>
Must never see: <internal ids, storage keys, paths, stack traces, jargon>
Product genre and the conventions they bring from it:
Identity guideline: <palette, type, tone, motion, once variants are chosen>
```
