# Example 5. Scope control

Mid-MEV on the learning platform (example 2), the sales lead asks for "a
leaderboard of learners, it will motivate them".

```markdown
Change: a leaderboard of learners by completed courses
Requested by: sales lead
Moves the metric? The metric is "HR finds and assigns training in under 2 minutes"; a leaderboard does not move it
Kind: new idea (not a correction, not a basic expectation of a capability in the cut)
Size: 2 slices
Decision by the outcome owner: post-MEV backlog
Reason: no effect on the metric; adds a privacy question (learners ranked in front of colleagues)
```

Had it been a correction, such as HR not finding the assign button, it
would be a basic expectation of a capability in the cut and would enter the
MEV as rework.
