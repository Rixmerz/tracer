# Audience profile

One per audience. A learning platform has at least two: learners and
administrators.

```markdown
Audience:
Who they are:
What they are trying to get done:
What they know:
What they do not know: <for example: CDN, UUID, endpoint, file path>
Their words: <labels and terms the interface must use>
Context: <devices, frequency, connection, time pressure>
Urgency: <how fast they need an answer, and what happens if they wait>
In five seconds they look for: <the one thing that decides whether they stay>
Accessibility needs: <default WCAG 2.2 AA, plus anything specific>
Must never see: <internal ids, storage keys, paths, stack traces, jargon>
Product genre and the conventions they bring from it:
Representatives: <3-5 real people from this audience, not the sponsor>
Identity guideline: <palette, type, tone, motion, once variants are chosen>
```

Light mode, for a small product such as a one-page site: one line per field
is enough, but the profiles are still written before the first mock, and the
mock is still read through them (`/tracer:empathy`). A site for a lawyer has
at least the person in an emergency, the person comparing, the company and
the visitor who was referred.
