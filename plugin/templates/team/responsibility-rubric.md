# Responsibility rubric

R is the highest factor, not the average. Set by the PO and the LT together,
never by the executor. Review the rubric against incidents each quarter.

```markdown
| Factor | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Money or operation | None | Minor inconvenience | A team's work slows | A process stops | Revenue, billing or operation stops |
| People affected | Internal, few | Internal, many | One client segment | Most clients | All clients, or vulnerable people |
| Legal or data | None | Internal data | Personal data read | Personal data written | Legal or regulatory duty |
| Reversibility | Minutes | Hours | With effort | Partially | Cannot be undone |
| Blocks others | Nobody | Own cell | One team | Several teams | A release or a client commitment |

Card: <id> · Scores: <money, people, legal, reversibility, blocks> · R = max = <n>
Set by: <PO>, <LT> on <date>
```
